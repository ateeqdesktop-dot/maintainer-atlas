# Code of Conduct

Maintainer Atlas welcomes respectful, constructive, and inclusive collaboration. Participants should assume good faith, focus on the technical problem rather than the person, and make space for different backgrounds and experience levels.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate disclosure of private information, sexualized conduct, and personal attacks are not acceptable. Maintainers may remove comments, close discussions, or restrict participation when necessary to keep the project safe and productive.

Report serious incidents privately to the repository maintainer. Reports will be handled discreetly and fairly.
